Sanctions Policy

Effective: February 2026

DappTestAudit complies with international sanctions regulations. This policy describes how the App implements regional restrictions to ensure compliance.

1. Regulatory Compliance

DappTestAudit is designed to comply with sanctions and export control regulations administered by:

  • OFAC — United States Office of Foreign Assets Control
  • EU — European Union Common Foreign and Security Policy sanctions
  • UK OFSI — United Kingdom Office of Financial Sanctions Implementation
  • UN — United Nations Security Council sanctions
  • Australian DFAT — Australian Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade sanctions

3. Detection Method

Regional detection is performed entirely on-device using multiple signals:

  • SIM country code — the country code of the installed SIM card (MCC)
  • Network country code — the country reported by the mobile network operator
  • Device locale — the language and region settings configured on the device
  • Timezone — the device's configured timezone

4. Privacy-Preserving Approach

  • All checks are performed locally on the device
  • No location data is transmitted to any server
  • No GPS or precise location is used
  • No location data is stored or logged
  • The check runs only at app startup

5. Circumvention

Users are prohibited from using VPNs, proxy servers, SIM spoofing, or any other means to circumvent regional restrictions.
